Ongoing Research Projects
======  
**Insurance Mathematics: Flat Tontine Termination Design with Mortality Dependence**  
_Short Description_: Addressed the limitations of traditional insurance models by developing a discrete-time hazard model with Gaussian copula. Demonstrated that neglecting mortality correlation led to systemic payouts underestimation during termination events. (Coauthored with Busayasachee Puang-ngern, Narapong Srivisal and Seksan Kiatsupaibul)    
_Status_: **Re-submitted to Insurance: Mathematics and Economics**  
_Current Draft_: [Working Paper](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6110191)   
**Market Microstructure: Bedtime Stories or Market Reality? How Overnight Information Shapes Next-Day Trading in Stock Exchange of Thailand**  
_Short Description_: Extended HAR-RRV model to examine the information transmission mechanism from overnight periods to day-time trading volatility. Stocks with lower trading costs process the directional value of overnight news more efficiently, whereas those with high retail net buys exhibit greater sensitivities to overnight information. (Coauthored with Wasin Siwasarit and Sicha Thubdimphun)    
_Status_: **Presented at Singapore Economic Review Conference (SERC, 2026) and Western Economic Association International (WEAI, 2026)**    
_Current Draft_: (Available upon Requests)  
**Machine Learning: Deep Cascading Threshold for Contextual Regression**  
_Short Description_: Proposed DeepCasT, an interpretable neural network architecture by explicitly decoupling context identification from contextual regression, requiring significantly fewer parameters than standard neural network architectures. (Coauthored with Nattapoom Praphudtham, Seksan Kiatsupaibul and Pakawan Chansiripas)    
_Status_: **Presented at International Conference on Machine Learning and Machine Intelligence (MLMI 2026)**

Past Research Projects (Master \& Bachelor Thesis)  
======  
**Cash Profitability in Corporate Liquidity Management with Cross-sectional Heterogeneity**  
_Short Description_: Extended the stylized facts of Almeida et al. (2005) by showing that firms accumulated higher proportions of cash in response to higher profitability. Consistent with Tirole (2010) corporate liquidity framework, higher profitability reduces manager-investor agency costs, thereby incentivizing investments **(MSc Management Sci at Peking University; under supervision of Ehsan Bolandifar)**      
_Current Draft_: [Working Paper](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1QgJ5CRA7IyAUZgc_vn-Mpeo3GTaybuFW/view?usp=sharing)  
**An Application of Reinforcement Learning to Credit Scoring Based on the Logistic Bandit Framework**  
_Short Description_: Formulated credit scoring and underwriting as a unified sequential decision problem using the Thompson sampling framework from Russo et al. (2018). Showed that the exploration mechanism enables Thompson sampling to outperform traditional approaches. **(MSc Statistics at Chulalongkorn University; under supervision of Seksan Kiatsupaibul)**    
_Status_: Best Presentation Award at International Conference on Applied Statistics (2022)  
_Current Draft_: [Working Paper](https://digital.car.chula.ac.th/chulaetd/6050/)  
_Conference Paper_: [Conference Paper](http://icas2022.stat.kmutnb.ac.th/doc/ICAS2022%20Proceeding.pdf)  
**Investigating the Role of Growth in Determining Capital Structure in Thai Stock Market (SET) through Signaling Game**  
_Short Description_: Developed a static signaling game under asymmetric information between managers and investors to show how a firm’s growth prospects positively determine its leverage ratio. Documented supporting empirical evidence consistent with pecking-order theory. **(Bachelor Thesis at Thammasat University; under supervision of Pornthep Benyaapikul and Sicha Thubdimphun)**  
_Status_: **Published in Thailand and the World Economy Journal, Vol. 39, No. 1 (2021)**  
_Publication Paper_: [Publication Paper](https://so05.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/TER/article/view/243081)
